# Hailstone QoL

A Hailstone add-on with small quality-of-life improvements for the **Modern UI**.

## Install

In Hailstone's login-screen **Add-ons** panel, paste `https://github.com/allhopeislost/hailstone-qol` and choose **Install**.

## Features

- **Target levels and class**: shows the level (and optionally the class) of NPCs on nameplates and in the target window. 
- **Spell display**: a richer spell window with readable effects (every effect has a readable name; the value is the raw spell value), casting details, spell IDs and damage per mana (raw spell values: no focus, resist, partials or crits; area spells show the per-target value). A proc or trigger row also says what the fired spell does, for example `Prismatic Strike II: 45 fire damage`.
- **Item display**: a richer item window with extra information such as base and tribute value, delay/damage ratio and lore.
- **Spell info in the item display**: an item's focus, proc, worn, click or scroll spell, with its effects and casting details laid out as in the spell display.
- **Other item versions** (Project Triune only, off by default): buttons to open the Enchanted or Legendary version of an item.
- **Target line on map and minimap**: draws a line from you to your target on the large map and on the minimap.
- **All NPCs on the map**: shows every living NPC on the maps.
- **FindWalk**: `/findW` (or Plugins → Find in the menu) opens a window that lists the spawns in the zone and you can navigate to them
- **Named tracker**: `/named` (or Plugins → Named in the menu) opens a window with the named NPCs of the zone you are in
- **Pet chain**: Keeps sending your pets down your extended
  target list after a pet attack, until the list is empty or you order them back.
  
Every feature can be switched on or off under **Options → Plugins**.

Every QoL window closes with **Esc**, like the client's own windows. Right-click a window's title bar to switch
"Close with Esc" off for that window.
